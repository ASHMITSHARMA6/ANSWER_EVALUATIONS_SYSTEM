Real-world scenario:

1. Teacher gives exam on "Photosynthesis"
   
2. Student: John submits handwritten answer sheet
   
3. Teacher: Takes photo of John's answer
   "john_answer.jpg"
   
4. Teacher: Goes to /ocr-handwritten page
   
5. Teacher: Uploads john_answer.jpg
   
6. System: Extracts text automatically
   "Photosynthesis is the process where plants
    convert light energy to chemical energy..."
   
7. Teacher: Reviews extracted text (looks good ✓)
   
8. Teacher: Enters model answer with key points:
   • Definition
   • Light-dependent reactions
   • Light-independent reactions
   • Role of chlorophyll
   • Energy (ATP/NADPH) production
   
9. Teacher: Clicks "Evaluate"
   
10. System: Evaluates and returns:
    Score: 72/100
    ✓ Matched: [photosynthesis, light, energy]
    ✗ Missing: [reactions, chlorophyll, ATP]
    Feedback: "Good basics. Study dark reactions..."
    
11. Teacher: Can now see John's strengths/weaknesses
    
12. Student: Receives feedback and improves
